DAILY PRACTICE TIPS
================================================================================

1. READING ALOUD
   - Read each sentence 2-3 times slowly
   - Then read naturally at normal conversation speed
   - Focus on correct pronunciation

2. RECORDING & PLAYBACK
   - Record yourself saying the sentences
   - Compare your pronunciation with native speakers
   - Identify areas for improvement

3. SPACED REPETITION
   - Practice 50 sentences per day
   - Rotate through all three categories weekly
   - Review previous days' sentences

4. CONTEXTUAL PRACTICE
   - Use office sentences during work
   - Practice informal sentences with colleagues
   - Use formal phrases in meetings

5. VARIATION
   - Change the context (who you're talking to, situation)
   - Practice with different emotions (formal vs. friendly)
   - Add your own sentences based on your daily interactions

6. FLUENCY BUILDING
   - Start slowly, increase speed gradually
   - Practice linking words together smoothly
   - Work on natural stress and intonation

7. CONVERSATION PARTNERS
   - Practice with colleagues if possible
   - Use language exchange apps
   - Record and share with English speakers for feedback

8. ACTIVE USE
   - Use these sentences in real conversations
   - Adapt them to your actual workplace situations
   - Create variations for different contexts
